# Candidate exact-period reduction and reuse argument

Author-side proof, pending independent validation. All residues and spans
refer to the same nonempty integer unit-slope cyclic observer as the paper.
No claim of a new generic minimum-cover or string-period algorithm.

Let Q_d be canonical and H_d(phi)=min_(r,v in Q_d)
[((phi-r-1) mod d)-v]. For e|d, H_d has period e exactly when Q_d is
invariant under (r,v)->((r+e) mod d,v). Necessity follows from the explicit
reset inverse: reset positions and their heights must repeat under the
translation. Sufficiency follows directly from permuting the kernel terms.
The period subgroup of the cyclic group has a least positive generator
e|d. Sorting resets, the cyclic word of (span, next-reset gap) repeats after
h terms iff this translation preserves Q_d, with e equal to the sum of those
h gaps. A standard minimal-word-period computation therefore finds e without
materializing all d phases. This is a representation-specific conclusion.

For such e, each term of Q_e=kappa_e Pi_e Q_d lifts to d/e terms
(r+ke,v), 0<=k<d/e. Their minimum cyclic remainder equals the remainder
modulo e, so this lift represents H_d. By the reset inverse and uniqueness,
the lifted canonical set is exactly Q_d. Thus the full master is recoverable
from (d,e,Q_e) only when this exact-lift binding has been checked, or otherwise
proved by the producer; an arbitrary projected Q_e is insufficient.

For a physical query P let m=gcd(P,d), h=gcd(e,m)=gcd(P,e). Because m|d,
the d/e translated residues exhaust each coset generated by e modulo m:
the orbit has m/h members and m/h divides d/e. Minimizing a modulo-m
remainder over that coset leaves its modulo-h remainder, with the same span.
Consequently the projected exact query profile equals the lift of
kappa_h Pi_h Q_e, and recurrent slack is (K-1)P+1+H_h(phi).
The physical P is never replaced in the load term. The stored period
metadata and exact-lift proof are extra information absent from the old
nondivisor counterexample. The old theorem remains valid for arbitrary
projected records without this information. Every fresh-policy prefix and
initialization/revocation condition remains a separate obligation.

The fundamental period minimizes the pair count among exact period-lift
records for this same H_d: every other admitted period is a multiple of e,
and its reset inverse contains that many copies of Q_e. This does not prove
minimum arbitrary encoding, minimum for sign-only queries or a hardware win.
Many profiles have e=d and yield no saving; added metadata can make their
complete records larger. All such cases must remain in the comparison.
