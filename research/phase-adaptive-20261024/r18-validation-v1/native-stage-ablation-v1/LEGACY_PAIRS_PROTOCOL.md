fixed-width COST design showed a small direct timeout-write effect. To
explain the earlier N32 full-plan improvement without attributing helper
perturbations to timeout writes, repeat matched apply/suppress controls in

only host application of the future-arm load is suppressed in the ablation.

contrasts, not an invariant-layout cost curve. The original8cycle apply

and suppress epochs must match exactly. Retain all signed outcomes.
